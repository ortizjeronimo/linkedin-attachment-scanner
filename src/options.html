VirusTotal lookup (optional)

Only the file's SHA-256 fingerprint is sent to VirusTotal, never the file itself. Get a free API key from your VirusTotal account. Free keys allow 4 lookups per minute.

Open in Google Drive (optional)

Adds an "Open in Google Drive" button to scan results. The file is uploaded to your own Google Drive and shown in Google's viewer, so it is never opened on this computer. The extension can only see files it uploaded itself, not the rest of your Drive, and nothing is uploaded unless you click the button.
